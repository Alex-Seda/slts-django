About Us

The Senior Living Truth Series is an educational seminar program focused on honest, practical information for aging adults and their families.

Our Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Living Truth Series is to educate, equip, and empower mature adults, caregivers, and families to make informed decisions about aging, senior living, and long-term planning.

What We Provide

The Senior Living Truth Series offers free educational seminars that address the real questions people face as they age. Topics include housing transitions, emergency preparedness, financial considerations, fraud awareness, and maintaining quality of life.

These seminars are informational in nature. We do not sell products or services, and we do not require participation beyond attending and learning.

Our Hosts

The Senior Living Truth Series is hosted by Becky and Roberto Seda. They oversee the program and work to ensure each seminar remains focused on clarity, accuracy, and respect for the audience. Their goal is to provide a welcoming environment where attendees can learn without pressure or obligation.

Program History

The Senior Living Truth Series was originally founded by Nicki Buckelew, Ph.D. and Chris Buckelew. While they are no longer involved in running the program, their early efforts helped establish its commitment to community education and honest discussion.

Why This Matters

Decisions related to aging can be complex and overwhelming. The Senior Living Truth Series exists to reduce confusion, encourage thoughtful planning, and support individuals and families with clear, trustworthy information.
