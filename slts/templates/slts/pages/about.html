About the Senior Living Truth Series

The Senior Living Truth Series exists to educate, empower, and equip mature adults and their families with honest, practical information about later-life choices — without any sales pitches.

Every month, we offer free seminars and workshops tailored specifically for seniors and lifelong learners. Topics include emergency planning, aging in place, navigating senior living options, managing finances, and understanding modern technology.

Our goal is simple: provide clear, truthful insight so attendees can make informed decisions with confidence.
