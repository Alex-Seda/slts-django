Senior Living Truth Series

About the Senior Living Truth Series

Our Purpose

Our Mission

The Senior Living Truth Series exists because the senior living space is confusing, sales-driven, and high-stakes.

Our mission is to provide clear, unbiased education so Oklahoma City seniors and their families can make informed decisions with dignity, confidence, and peace of mind.

We believe:

  • You deserve the full picture, not just the parts that benefit someone else
  • Planning ahead is always better than reacting in crisis
  • The best decisions come from understanding your options, not from pressure tactics
  • Every family should know what programs and resources are actually available to them

We're here for those who believe in having Plan A, Plan B, and Plan C — who want to look to the future with an "I can do this" attitude instead of fear. Because when you're prepared, everything changes.

Meet Your Hosts

Becky Seda

Roberto Seda

When Becky and Roberto Seda were given the opportunity to continue Senior Living Truth Series, they said yes because they saw it as a natural extension of their mission to educate, equip, and advocate for Oklahoma families.

They believe that good decisions begin with good information and that families deserve a place where they can learn, ask questions, and explore their options without pressure or sales tactics.

That belief continues to guide Senior Living Truth Series today.

Through years of serving hundreds of families during some of life's biggest transitions, Becky and Roberto have seen firsthand the questions, fears, and uncertainty that often accompany aging. Whether someone is deciding how to safely remain in their home, helping a parent navigate the next chapter, understanding legal and financial considerations, or exploring senior living options, they know that having trusted information at the right time can change everything.

As owners of Senior Living Truth Series, they are committed to creating a community where older adults and their families can hear from respected local professionals, gain practical knowledge, and make confident decisions based on truth, not pressure.

Together, their professional experience provides a unique perspective on the decisions families face. Becky's background in residential real estate, probate, trust and estate sales, senior housing transitions, and downsizing, combined with Roberto's work in estate planning and probate law, allows them to guide families through the housing, legal, and planning decisions that so often intersect with the aging journey.

More importantly, those experiences have reinforced a shared belief: education should come before decisions, and every family deserves trusted professionals who will help them understand their options before they're forced to make them.

Becky and Roberto are passionate about serving the Oklahoma City community and are raising their three sons with the same commitment to work ethic, integrity, and service that shapes every Senior Living Truth Series event.

Our Difference

What Makes Us Different

Senior Living Truth Series is different by design. We bring together experts in housing, legal, financial, healthcare, and logistics—because no single industry has all the answers.

Senior living decisions don’t happen in isolation. By connecting these perspectives, we help you understand how choices in one area impact everything else.

We also talk about what others avoid: emotional transitions, real tradeoffs, and long-term consequences that don’t show up in brochures.

Every seminar features trusted Oklahoma City professionals—not salespeople. There’s no pitching, no pressure, and no steering toward a single solution.

We’re not here to tell you what to do. We’re here to give you the clarity to decide for yourself.

Original Founders

Dr. Nikki Buckelew, Ph.D.

Chris Buckelew

In 2016, as local business owners, advocates, and family caregivers, Chris and Dr. Nikki Buckelew, Ph.D., organized an annual series of educational programs they titled the Senior Living Truth Series. First held at the Northwest Library in Oklahoma City, the goal was to offer unbiased education about the issues associated with aging, without a sales pitch attached. Collaborating with other local educationally minded professionals, the series quickly grew from only a few attendees to over 100 each month. They outgrew the library and as attendance increased, they relocated to various locations throughout the metro including the MAPS 3 Healthy Living Center, Quail Creek Country Club, Crossings Community Church, and eventually, Francis Tuttle Technical Center.

In 2018, after gaining attention from other organizations and professionals around the country, Chris and Nikki began helping other civic leaders develop similar programs in their own communities across several states and in Canada. The Oklahoma City–based Senior Living Truth Series continues to serve as a model for others who share the same commitment to educate, equip, and empower mature adults to make informed decisions on their own terms.

In 2025, Chris and Nikki passed leadership of the series to Becky and Roberto Seda, confident in their shared commitment to keeping the program rooted in honest, relevant, and unbiased education.
