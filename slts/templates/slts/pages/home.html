Meet Your Hosts

Senior Living Truth Series is hosted by Becky and Roberto Seda, Oklahoma City residents committed to giving seniors and families clear, honest information for life-changing decisions.

Through their work in real estate, move coordination, and elder law, they’ve guided hundreds of local families and seen firsthand that people are often shown only the options being sold to them.

In 2025, they stepped into ownership of the Senior Living Truth Series to change that—creating free, expert-led seminars moderated by Becky that cover housing, healthcare, financial, and legal planning.

This isn’t a sales pitch. It’s honest, practical education designed to help Oklahoma City seniors plan ahead and make confident decisions from clarity, not crisis.
